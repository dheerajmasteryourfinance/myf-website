Fix billing discipline and rate integrity

Set a firm write-down approval process. Every discount over 5% needs partner sign-off. Track realisation by fee earner monthly so patterns become visible.

Tighten your WIP billing cycle

Raise invoices within 7 days of matter completion. Set calendar triggers and billing review checkpoints, not ad-hoc billing runs.
